## CEM-FAQ-002｜What happens if I make a late payment? / What should I be aware of when paying after the due date?

- FAQ ID: CEM-FAQ-002
- Language: English
- Question: What happens if I make a late payment? / What should I be aware of when paying after the due date?

**Answer**

If the customer does not pay the electricity bill on or before the Payment Due Date and the bill amount exceeds MOP 50, a late payment surcharge equal to 3.5% of the outstanding amount, or a minimum of MOP 20, will be charged. If payment is still not made by the Disconnection Avoidance Date, the electricity supply will be suspended. Supply can only be restored after all outstanding charges have been settled, and the next bill will also include the late payment surcharge and an electricity reconnection fee of MOP 100.

## CEM-FAQ-021｜What is the multiplying factor?

- FAQ ID: CEM-FAQ-021
- Language: English
- Question: What is the multiplying factor?

**Answer**

Some meters record only a fraction of the electricity consumed, so a multiplying factor is required to determine the actual consumption. Electricity consumption is therefore calculated by subtracting the previous month’s reading from the current month’s reading and multiplying the result by the multiplying factor.

## CEM-FAQ-024｜How are this month’s charges calculated?

- FAQ ID: CEM-FAQ-024
- Language: English
- Question: How are this month’s charges calculated?

**Answer**

This month’s charges are the sum of the demand charge, consumption charge, Tariff Clause Adjustment and Government Tax. The demand charge and Government Tax are calculated according to the subscribed demand, while the consumption charge and Tariff Clause Adjustment are calculated according to the electricity consumption recorded by the meter.
